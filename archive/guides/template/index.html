Build your own budget

  1. Make a copy and remove the example rows from Transactions, Categories, Budget Plan, Accounts, Balance History and AutoCat when you are ready to add your own data. Keep the headers.
  2. Enter transactions and tag business categories on Categories. Add a year, month and category target to Budget Plan. Both budget views recalculate.
  3. For automatic updates, connect the workbook to your ledger and map the accounts you want to refresh.
