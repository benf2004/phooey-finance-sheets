A mixed purchase, from entry to estimate

  1. Import the purchase into a checking or card account.
  2. Open the Phooey sidebar and split the amount across business and personal categories. Assign the personal portion an excluded tax treatment in Sheets setup when appropriate.
  3. Refresh the workbook. Monthly cash flow and the quarterly estimate use the same ledger as the dashboard.
